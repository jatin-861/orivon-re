# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative founders, product teams, startup executives, and design managers seeking a high-fidelity, custom-crafted design studio to build digital projects.

## Product Purpose

To showcase Orivon's design craft, methodology, selected projects, pricing, and services to convert prospective clients into design partners.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, typographic, confident, warm, human-made, and aesthetic-focused.

## Anti-references

AI-generated template layouts, standard SaaS template grids with centered hero and three feature cards, purple/blue gradient glows, Inter-based default layout styling, and generic 3D meshes/spinners.

## Design Principles

1. Typography First: Structure layouts around custom font scales (Cabinet Grotesk, Bodoni Moda) and precise visual hierarchy rather than decoration.
2. Storytelling Pacing: Reject templates to guide users through custom scroll narratives.
3. Operational Warmth & Honesty: Ground the brand in real people, physical locations, and transparent methodology.
4. Tactile Interactions: Use micro-animations, active press scaling, and interactive physics backdrops that respond to user cadence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA color contrast, reduced motion support (via `useReducedMotion`), clean keyboard navigation, and semantic HTML layout tags.
